# Exact same-ingredient lower-price replacement

The administrator Recipe adjustments screen now compares only approved products whose normalized product names are exactly identical. Similar, related, or product-family matches are excluded.

Administrators can search the Approved Product List by product name, APL code, or division. Lower-price opportunities can also be searched independently. Each opportunity has a checkbox, and only selected products are changed after confirmation.

The comparison still requires the same normalized unit (kg, litre, or each) and a lower normalized price. Recipe quantities, units, notes, factors, portions, GP settings, and other recipe data remain unchanged. Every applied replacement is retained in the audit history.
