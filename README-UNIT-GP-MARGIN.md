# Unit-specific desired GP margin

The recipe details page now displays an editable **Desired GP margin %** beside **Required portions** for every authenticated role: unit user, unit manager, head office and administrator.

- The margin is stored by recipe and unit.
- Users in different units may save different targets for the same recipe.
- The recipe's master/default margin is used until a unit saves its own value.
- Saving a unit margin does not change ingredient quantities, purchase prices, recipe costs or the master recipe.
- The required selling price per portion is recalculated from the selected portions and unit margin.
- Values are limited to 0.00% through 99.99%.
