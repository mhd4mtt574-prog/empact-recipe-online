# Compliance Tracker Scope Update

Allergens and Recipe Adjustments have been removed from compliance measurement.

The compliance tracker now scores only:
- Resale Pricing
- Recipe Costing
- Reports & Tie-back
- Food Waste
- Promotions

Allergens and Recipe Adjustments remain available as app functions, but they do not affect overall compliance, module charts, unit heatmaps, status bands, regional scores, or trend calculations.
