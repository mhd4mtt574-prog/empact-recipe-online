# Region-specific recipes and APL pricing

- Unit users now receive recipe ingredient products and prices from their own region only.
- Gauteng maps to the JHB APL, KwaZulu-Natal maps to KZN, and Cape Town/Western Cape maps to CT.
- Recipe quantities, methods, portions and GP settings remain shared while the APL product, code, pack and price are resolved per region.
- Exact-name regional product mappings are stored per ingredient and region. Missing regional matches are shown without a cross-region APL code or price.
- Recipe Adjustments now includes a region selector. Product search, exact-product replacement and lower-price comparison are restricted to the selected region.
- Lower-price comparisons never compare prices across regions.
