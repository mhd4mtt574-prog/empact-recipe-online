# Regional Approved Product List — July 2026

This version imports the complete CT, JHB and KZN tabs from `APL Extract July 2026.xlsx`.

- CT (Cape Town): 8,878 supplier/product lines
- JHB (Johannesburg): 11,448 supplier/product lines
- KZN (KwaZulu-Natal): 6,467 supplier/product lines
- Total imported: 26,793 lines

Administrator accounts are assigned to Cape Town Unit and open the CT APL by default. The APL screen includes regional tabs, search, status filtering, 100-row pagination, supplier and contract details, and cost updating.

The original workbook tab `CTN` is displayed in the app as `CT`.
