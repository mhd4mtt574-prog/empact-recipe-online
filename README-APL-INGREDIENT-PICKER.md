# APL Ingredient Picker

The Create Recipe and Edit Recipe pages now include a **Search APL Ingredients** button beside the Ingredients and Costing heading.

Administrators can search active products in Cape Town, Gauteng, or KwaZulu-Natal, select multiple products, and add them directly to the costing grid. Product code, description, pack size, purchase price, factor, and unit of measure are populated from the APL. Existing product codes are disabled in the search results to prevent duplicates. Linked APL product and pricing cells are read-only in the browser; standard quantity and cooking notes remain editable.
