# Regional allergen declarations

- Approved products can record **Contains**, **May contain**, and **Supplier label verification required**.
- Recipe views combine the recipe-level declaration with allergen data from the approved products resolved for the logged-in unit region.
- Gauteng, Cape Town and KwaZulu-Natal may therefore display different allergen declarations when their approved regional products differ.
- Administrators maintain product allergen data in the Approved Product List.
- Unit users see the declaration on recipe details and printouts.
- Supplier verification warnings remain visible for compound or unverified products.
